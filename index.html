THE OPERATIONAL CRISIS

DENGUE FORECASTS FAIL SILENTLY.

Municipal response teams have finite chemical adulticide, larvicide, and staff. When clinic reporting breaks down, standard ML models output confident numbers based on garbage inputs.

01 · DELAYED REPORTING
Clinic test backlogs dump 3 weeks of dengue cases into a single Monday batch. Models interpret this as a sudden epidemic explosion.
02 · INCOMPLETE SURVEILLANCE
Public holidays or doctor strikes cause zero-case entries. Point models assume the outbreak vanished and demobilize field teams.
03 · TOO COARSE
District-level aggregate warnings fail to tell vector control workers which specific city streets need ultra-low-volume thermal fogging.
